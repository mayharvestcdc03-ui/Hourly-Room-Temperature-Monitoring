3. Counter Checking (per Area)

Counter checking is an independent check of the room temperature. The counter checker reads the temperature of the area using his/her own thermometer, and the system compares it with the latest hourly monitoring reading of the same area. A difference of more than is flagged as a discrepancy. The counter checker must be a different person from the one who monitored. Fill in the details, then type the counter check reading beside each room – the °C and the time are added automatically. Records are synced across devices.

4. Weekly Quality Verification

Quality personnel verify the monitoring records of one whole week (Monday to Sunday) in a single review. Compare the readings with the standards, check the deviations and remarks, then sign. Every pending record in the selected week is marked with your decision. Night shift records follow the advance date of the sheet.
